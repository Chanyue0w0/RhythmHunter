# Otter Aquarium Prototype scenes

- `OtterAquarium.unity` is the top-down sea otter movement, surface transition, slide, and water VFX prototype.
- `OtterAquariumCombat.unity` is the aquarium-owned continuation of the original 3v3 rhythm combat scene.
- The combat scene intentionally contains no pirate ocean wave, ocean surface, or ship motion systems.
- `Assets/FightDemo/Scenes/FightScene.unity` remains as a legacy reference. Continue future scene work in this folder.
